What We Do

Wood. Pipe. Ag.
Gates. Staining.

From a backyard privacy fence to miles of agricultural wire — we install it all across South Central Missouri with the same crew, same quality, every time.

Privacy & Wood Fence

Cedar, pine, or treated wood — we build and install privacy fencing that looks great and holds up through Missouri winters.

Chain Link Fence

Residential and commercial chain link — galvanized or vinyl-coated. Durable, low maintenance, and priced right.

Pipe & Panel Fence

Heavy-duty pipe fence and livestock panels for cattle, horses, and hogs. Built to hold and look good doing it.

Agricultural Fencing

Barbed wire, woven wire, high-tensile — we fence pastures, fields, and property lines. Big acreage is no problem.

Gate Automation & Welding

Custom gate entry systems, automated openers, and welded steel gates. We build and install — manual or automated, single or double.

Staining & Refinishing

We stain and refinish wood privacy fences to protect and restore them. Proper staining extends the life of your fence and keeps it looking sharp for years.

About Holliday W&F

Salem's Fence Crew

Nick runs every job personally. When you call, you're talking to the guy who walks your property, gives you a straight number, and shows up with the crew. No call centers, no hand-offs.
