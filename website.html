What We Do

Complete Fencing & Welding Solutions

From residential backyards to sprawling farms, we build and restore fences that stand the test of time. Quality materials, expert installation, and craftsmanship you'll see in every post.

🌲

Privacy & Wood Fence

Beautiful, durable privacy fencing for your home or property. We work with cedar, pine, and composite materials to match your style and budget.

  • 6ft & 8ft privacy panels
  • Dog-ear & flat-top styles
  • Custom staining & finishing
  • Residential & commercial
⛓️

Chain Link Fence

Durable, low-maintenance chain link for security, sports, and perimeter fencing. Galvanized or vinyl-coated — built to last decades.

  • Residential & commercial
  • Security topping options
  • Multiple heights available
  • Gates & access points
🔩

Pipe & Panel Fence

Heavy-duty pipe and panel fencing — ideal for equestrian, livestock, and properties that demand maximum strength and longevity.

  • Horse & livestock safe
  • Custom welded panels
  • Corral & arena work
  • Powder coat finishing
🌾

Agricultural Fencing

Barbed wire, woven wire, hi-tensile, and electric fencing solutions for farms, ranches, and large properties across Iowa.

  • Barbed & woven wire
  • Hi-tensile electric
  • T-post & wood post
  • Large acreage projects
🖌️

Fence Staining & Treatment

Extend the life of your existing fence with professional staining, bleaching, and UV-protective treatment. Restore that brand-new look.

  • Oil & water-based stains
  • Bleach & brightening
  • Spray & brush application
  • Multi-year protection
⚡

Custom Welding

From custom gates and decorative ironwork to structural welding and repairs — our certified welders handle any metalwork project.

  • Custom gates & entries
  • Structural repairs
  • Decorative metalwork
  • MIG, TIG & stick welding

Our Story

Iowa-Grown.
Family-Owned.

Holliday Welding & Fence has been serving Iowa families and landowners for over two decades. What started as a small welding shop has grown into one of the region's most trusted names in fencing and metalwork.

We believe every fence tells a story — and we take pride in building ones that last generations. Our crews are local, our materials are quality, and our word is our bond.
